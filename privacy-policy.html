Plain-language policy for App Store / public beta. This explains what Brook processes. It is not formal legal counsel — have a lawyer review if you expand markets or add paid features.

1. Overview

Brook is a personal nutrition logging app with an optional social layer called Nom Noms.

  • Diary & local data (meals, photos, preferences) stay on your device (SwiftData). Full diary iCloud sync is not enabled for free users.
  • Social / Nom Noms uses Apple CloudKit (public database in iCloud.app.hearth.001). You may claim a public PublicProfile handle (username) tied to your iCloud account — this is not Sign in with Apple and not a separate Brook password account.

This policy explains what data Brook may access or send, why, and your choices.

3. How we collect it

  • You enter it — food logs, captions, descriptions, searches, friend requests.
  • Device permissions — Camera, Photos, Location, Notifications, HealthKit. iOS asks; you can deny or later revoke in iOS Settings.
  • On-device storage — SwiftData / UserDefaults on your iPhone for diary and preferences.
  • CloudKit (Apple) — when you use Nom Noms / friends: PublicProfile, FeedPost / FeedIndex, friend request records, reactions, and optional Report records in the public CloudKit database for container iCloud.app.hearth.001. Apple authenticates you via iCloud; Brook does not see your Apple ID password.
  • Barcode lookups — product metadata from Open Food Facts when you scan/look up a product.
  • AI features — when you use meal photo analysis, describe-text, label OCR AI fallback, or food search AI, the relevant image and/or text may be sent to DeepSeek’s servers for processing.
  • Apple frameworks — HealthKit, MapKit, User Notifications, App Store distribution as provided by Apple.
  • AdMob SDK — may be present in the app binary for a future ads path, but ads are OFF and not initialized for this beta. See §5.

6. International transfers

When you use AI features, content may be processed by DeepSeek outside Hong Kong, including possibly in China. By using those features, you understand that transfer/processing may occur under DeepSeek’s infrastructure and practices. If you prefer not to send data abroad for AI, do not use those AI features (you can still log manually).

CloudKit data is processed by Apple according to Apple’s iCloud / CloudKit regions and practices for your Apple ID.

7. Retention & deletion

  • On device: Diary data lives on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes local Brook storage, subject to how iOS handles app deletion.
  • Nom Noms / PublicProfile: In Settings you can Delete my Brook profile (when you have a claimed handle). That best-effort deletes your PublicProfile and your FeedPosts / FeedIndex from CloudKit and clears the local claim. Some friend-request / inbox remnants may remain until cleaned by normal friend flows; contact us if you need help finishing a wipe.
  • Blocks: Stored on your device only; clearing Brook / reinstall clears them unless restored from a backup.
  • HealthKit: Data written to Apple Health remains under your Apple Health controls.
  • AI / third parties: Content sent for a request is processed to return a result. Third-party retention is governed by those providers’ policies.
  • Requests: Email feedback@brook.app for privacy questions.

8. Children’s privacy

Brook is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided data through Brook, contact feedback@brook.app.

9. Health data & advertising

  • HealthKit data is used only for Brook’s health/nutrition features you enable.
  • It is not used for advertising, marketing, or sale to data brokers.
  • AdMob may exist in the binary but is inactive for this beta. If ads return, health data still will not power ads.

10. Your choices & withdrawing consent

  • Deny or revoke Camera, Photos, Location, Notifications, Health in iOS Settings → Brook (and Health app sharing settings).
  • Stop using AI features that upload images/text.
  • Unshare / delete Nom Noms posts; delete your Brook profile from Settings; block or report others’ posts.
  • Delete the app to remove local Brook storage.
  • Contact feedback@brook.app.
