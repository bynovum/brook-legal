1. Overview

Brook is a personal nutrition logging app. For the friends beta, Brook does not create user accounts and does not use Sign in with Apple. Your logs, photos, and preferences stay on your device (SwiftData). iCloud sync is not shipping for this beta.

This policy explains what data Brook may access or send, why, and your choices. Plain English, short sections.

3. How we collect it

  • You enter it — food logs, edits, descriptions, searches.
  • Device permissions — Camera, Photos, Location, Notifications, HealthKit. iOS asks; you can deny or later revoke in iOS Settings.
  • On-device storage — SwiftData on your iPhone; no Brook cloud account for beta.
  • Barcode lookups — product metadata fetched from Open Food Facts when you scan/look up a product.
  • AI features — when you use meal photo analysis, describe-text, label OCR AI fallback, or food search AI, the relevant image and/or text may be sent to DeepSeek’s servers for processing.
  • Apple frameworks — HealthKit, MapKit, User Notifications, App Store distribution as provided by Apple.
  • AdMob SDK — may be present in the app binary for a future ads path, but ads are OFF and not initialized for the friends beta. See §5.

6. International transfers

When you use AI features, content may be processed by DeepSeek outside Hong Kong, including possibly in China. By using those features, you understand that transfer/processing may occur under DeepSeek’s infrastructure and practices. If you prefer not to send data abroad for AI, do not use those AI features (you can still log manually).

7. Retention & deletion

  • On device: Your Brook data lives on your iPhone for the beta. Deleting the app (or clearing Brook’s local data) removes local Brook storage, subject to how iOS handles app deletion.
  • HealthKit: Data written to Apple Health remains under your Apple Health controls; remove or manage it in the Health app / iOS Settings as Apple allows.
  • AI / third parties: Content sent for a request is processed to return a result. We do not operate a Brook cloud archive of your meals for beta. Third-party retention is governed by those providers’ policies.
  • Requests: Email cozkyle@gmail.com for privacy questions. Because beta data is primarily on-device, the practical way to delete Brook’s local data is to delete the app or clear it locally; we cannot remotely wipe a device we do not host.

8. Children’s privacy

Brook is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (US COPPA-style baseline). If you believe a child under 13 has provided data through Brook, contact cozkyle@gmail.com. Families in Hong Kong and elsewhere should also follow local age / parental-consent rules; you may raise the minimum age in the Terms if you expand markets later.

9. Health data & advertising

  • HealthKit data is used only for Brook’s health/nutrition features you enable.
  • It is not used for advertising, marketing, or sale to data brokers.
  • AdMob may exist in the binary but is inactive for friends beta. If ads return, health data still will not power ads.

10. Your choices & withdrawing consent

  • Deny or revoke Camera, Photos, Location, Notifications, Health in iOS Settings → Brook (and Health app sharing settings).
  • Stop using AI features that upload images/text.
  • Delete the app to remove local Brook storage.
  • Contact cozkyle@gmail.com with questions.
